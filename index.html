Campaign Title: Your Special Delivery Has Arrived: Jiji's Special Invitation to Ghibli
Role: Digital Marketing Strategy & Visual Concept Design
Project Type: Personal Concept Project
Target Brand: VIZ Media & Studio Ghibli Official US Pop-up

💡 Executive Summary

Background

Developed a character-focused marketing strategy to maximize local fans' desire to collect and generate viral buzz, timed with the first-ever official Studio Ghibli pop-up store opening in the US.

Key Differentiator
  • Selected "Jiji" from Kiki's Delivery Service as the beloved mascot with strong appeal to both mainstream and hardcore fans.
  • Applied an immersive storytelling concept where Jiji delivers a "special invitation" to guide US fans directly into the offline pop-up store, moving beyond simple merchandise sales.
    (*This campaign focuses on offline visits and experiences, and is unrelated to physical delivery/shipping services.)

🎯 Target & Audience Insight

Primary Target

US-based fans in their 20-30s of animation and Studio Ghibli, as well as enthusiasts of emotional healing IPs and cat characters.

Key Insight
  • A pop-up store is more than just a retail space; it is an experience of "stepping directly into the world of the film.
  • Utilizing Jiji loved for being Kiki's reliable companion and witty lines creates immediate familiarity and high collectibility for local fans.
